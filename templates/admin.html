ℹ️ Inventory Turnover Measurement

The Inventory Turnover Rate measures stock velocity—how efficiently products are sold and replaced relative to average stock levels.

Formula:
Turnover Rate = Units Sold ÷ Average Inventory

Where:
Average Inventory = [Current Stock + (Current Stock + Units Sold)] ÷ 2

💡 Interpretation: A higher ratio indicates strong sales velocity and minimal storage holding costs, while a lower ratio suggests overstocking or slow-moving items.
